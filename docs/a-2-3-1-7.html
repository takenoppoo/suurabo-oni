別冊練習問題 7 の解答　sinθ+cosθ=k から積・3乗の和・4乗の和

(1)　\(\sin\theta+\cos\theta=\dfrac{3}{5}\) のとき、\(\sin\theta\cos\theta,\ \sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta,\ \sin^4\theta+\cos^4\theta\) の値を求めよ。

両辺を2乗

\[(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2=1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\dfrac{9}{25}\]

積

\[\sin\theta\cos\theta=- \dfrac{8}{25}\]

3乗の和

\[\sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta=(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^3-3\sin\theta\cos\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)=\dfrac{27}{125}-3\cdot- \dfrac{8}{25}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}=\dfrac{99}{125}\]

4乗の和

\[\sin^4\theta+\cos^4\theta=1-2\sin^2\theta\cos^2\theta=\dfrac{497}{625}\]

【答案】

\[- \dfrac{8}{25},\ \dfrac{99}{125},\ \dfrac{497}{625}\]

答

(2)　\(\sin\theta+\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{3}\) のとき、\(\sin\theta\cos\theta,\ \sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta,\ \sin^4\theta+\cos^4\theta\) の値を求めよ。

両辺を2乗

\[(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2=1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{9}\]

積

\[\sin\theta\cos\theta=- \dfrac{4}{9}\]

3乗の和

\[\sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta=(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^3-3\sin\theta\cos\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)=\dfrac{1}{27}-3\cdot- \dfrac{4}{9}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{13}{27}\]

4乗の和

\[\sin^4\theta+\cos^4\theta=1-2\sin^2\theta\cos^2\theta=\dfrac{49}{81}\]

【答案】

\[- \dfrac{4}{9},\ \dfrac{13}{27},\ \dfrac{49}{81}\]

答

(3)　\(\sin\theta+\cos\theta=\dfrac{7}{5}\) のとき、\(\sin\theta\cos\theta,\ \sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta,\ \sin^4\theta+\cos^4\theta\) の値を求めよ。

両辺を2乗

\[(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2=1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\dfrac{49}{25}\]

積

\[\sin\theta\cos\theta=\dfrac{12}{25}\]

3乗の和

\[\sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta=(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^3-3\sin\theta\cos\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)=\dfrac{343}{125}-3\cdot\dfrac{12}{25}\cdot\dfrac{7}{5}=\dfrac{91}{125}\]

4乗の和

\[\sin^4\theta+\cos^4\theta=1-2\sin^2\theta\cos^2\theta=\dfrac{337}{625}\]

【答案】

\[\dfrac{12}{25},\ \dfrac{91}{125},\ \dfrac{337}{625}\]

答
