別冊練習問題 1 の解答　sinθ+cosθ の値から他の値を求める

(1)　\(0^\circ<\theta<180^\circ,\ \sin\theta+\cos\theta=\dfrac13\ \text{のとき},\ \sin\theta\cos\theta,\ \sin\theta-\cos\theta,\ \sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta\)

2乗：

\[1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\dfrac19\Rightarrow\sin\theta\cos\theta=-\dfrac49\]

\((\sin\theta-\cos\theta)^2=1+\dfrac89=\dfrac{17}9\)。\(\sin\theta>0,\ \cos\theta<0\) より \(\sin\theta-\cos\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt{17}}3\)。

\[\sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta=\dfrac1{27}-3\cdot\left(-\dfrac49\right)\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac1{27}+\dfrac49=\dfrac{1+12}{27}=\dfrac{13}{27}\]

【答案】

\[-\dfrac49,\ \ \dfrac{\sqrt{17}}3,\ \ \dfrac{13}{27}\]

答

(2)　\(0^\circ<\theta<180^\circ,\ \sin\theta+\cos\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt2}2\ \text{のとき},\ \sin\theta\cos\theta,\ \sin\theta-\cos\theta,\ \sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta\)

2乗：

\[1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\dfrac12\Rightarrow\sin\theta\cos\theta=-\dfrac14\]

\((\sin\theta-\cos\theta)^2=1+\dfrac12=\dfrac32\)。符号は正：\(\sin\theta-\cos\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt6}2\)。

\[\sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta=\left(\dfrac{\sqrt2}2\right)^3-3\cdot\left(-\dfrac14\right)\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt2}2=\dfrac{\sqrt2}4+\dfrac{3\sqrt2}8=\dfrac{5\sqrt2}8\]

【答案】

\[-\dfrac14,\ \ \dfrac{\sqrt6}2,\ \ \dfrac{5\sqrt2}8\]

答

(3)　\(0^\circ<\theta<180^\circ,\ \sin\theta+\cos\theta=-\dfrac15\ \text{のとき},\ \sin\theta\cos\theta,\ \sin\theta-\cos\theta,\ \sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta\)

2乗：

\[1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\dfrac1{25}\Rightarrow\sin\theta\cos\theta=-\dfrac{12}{25}\]

\((\sin\theta-\cos\theta)^2=1+\dfrac{24}{25}=\dfrac{49}{25}\)。符号は正：\(\sin\theta-\cos\theta=\dfrac75\)。

\[\sin^3\theta+\cos^3\theta=\left(-\dfrac15\right)^3-3\cdot\left(-\dfrac{12}{25}\right)\cdot\left(-\dfrac15\right)=-\dfrac1{125}-\dfrac{36}{125}=-\dfrac{37}{125}\]

（確かめ：\(\sin\theta=\dfrac35,\ \cos\theta=-\dfrac45\)）。

【答案】

\[-\dfrac{12}{25},\ \ \dfrac75,\ \ -\dfrac{37}{125}\]
