別冊練習問題 6 の解答　∞−∞ 型の無理式の極限

(1)　\(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{n^2+6n+2}-n\right)\) を求めよ。

有理化

\[\sqrt{n^2+6n+2}-n=\dfrac{(n^2+6n+2)-n^2}{\sqrt{n^2+6n+2}+n}=\dfrac{6n+2}{\sqrt{n^2+6n+2}+n}\]

\(n\) で割る

\[=\dfrac{6+\dfrac{2}{n}}{\sqrt{1+\dfrac{6}{n}+\dfrac{2}{n^2}}+1}\]

極限

\[\to\dfrac{6}{1+1}=3\]

【答案】

\[3\]

(2)　\(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{n^2+2n+5}-n\right)\) を求めよ。

有理化

\[\sqrt{n^2+2n+5}-n=\dfrac{(n^2+2n+5)-n^2}{\sqrt{n^2+2n+5}+n}=\dfrac{2n+5}{\sqrt{n^2+2n+5}+n}\]

\(n\) で割る

\[=\dfrac{2+\dfrac{5}{n}}{\sqrt{1+\dfrac{2}{n}+\dfrac{5}{n^2}}+1}\]

極限

\[\to\dfrac{2}{1+1}=1\]

【答案】

\[1\]

答

(3)　\(\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\sqrt{n^2+10n+3}-n\right)\) を求めよ。

有理化

\[\sqrt{n^2+10n+3}-n=\dfrac{(n^2+10n+3)-n^2}{\sqrt{n^2+10n+3}+n}=\dfrac{10n+3}{\sqrt{n^2+10n+3}+n}\]

\(n\) で割る

\[=\dfrac{10+\dfrac{3}{n}}{\sqrt{1+\dfrac{10}{n}+\dfrac{3}{n^2}}+1}\]

極限

\[\to\dfrac{10}{1+1}=5\]

【答案】

\[5\]
